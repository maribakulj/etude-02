# State of the art for v0.4 — visual correspondence and copy detection (2026-10-13)

Targeted check done for the v0.4 reframing (subagent, verified references only; pirate sites not
used). Summary: **no work evaluates automatic visual matching against an expert-documented copying
history across the editions of an illustrated printed book, and none asks whether a new design
follows the text or the previous picture.** Building blocks exist separately.

## The ground truth: Tung's *Variorum*

Preface read in full ([PDF](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/tung/alciatotungedition-000.pdf)).
212 emblems × 9 programmes:

| Code | Edition | Glasgow witness |
|---|---|---|
| 31 | Augsburg 1531 (Breu workshop) | A31b (A31a, A34a: same Steyner chain) |
| 34 | Paris 1534 (Jollat?) | A34b |
| 46 | Venice, Aldus 1546 | none found in the related-emblem lists |
| 47 | Lyon, de Tournes 1547 (Bernard Salomon), **reproduced by Tung from the 1556 edition** | A56a (de Tournes and Gazeau 1556), block identity to check |
| 51 | Lyon, Rouille/Bonhomme 1551 | A51a (A50a closely similar) |
| 67 | Frankfurt 1567 (Amman/Solis) | A67a |
| 77 | Antwerp, Plantin 1577 (van der Borcht), **reproduced by Tung from Leiden 1591** | A91a |
| 83 | Paris, Marnef/Cavellat 1583 | FALc (Paris 1584, bilingual), identity to check |
| 21 | Padua, Tozzi 1621 | A21a |

Tung's relations are **prose**, not codes ("This design is followed in reverse by 51 but in the
same direction by 67"; "A new design illustrating the first half of the text is in 47/83…"). A
structured graph has to be extracted and its reliability measured. Tung also says, for some new
designs, **which part of the epigram they illustrate**: the label needed for the "text or previous
picture" question.

## Nearest works

- **Kaoua, Shen, Durr, Lazaris, Picard, Aubry, "Image Collation: Matching illustrations in
  manuscripts"**, ICDAR 2021, LNCS: 351–366, [DOI 10.1007/978-3-030-86337-1_24](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-86337-1_24),
  [arXiv 2108.08109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.08109).
  - Data: 6 manuscripts of 2 texts, more than 2,000 illustrations, 1,200 annotated
    correspondences.
  - Results: mAP rises from 35–46 % with raw features to 82–89 % with normalisation and cycle
    consistency.
  - Limits: the task is *which illustration corresponds to which*, not the type of copy; mirroring
    is not handled.
- **Chung, Arandjelović, Bergel, Franklin, Zisserman, "Re-presentations of Art Collections"**,
  ECCV Workshops 2014: 85–100, [DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-16178-5_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-16178-5_6).
  English broadside ballads; an SVM on RANSAC geometry and inking separates **same block from
  copied block** (100 % precision at 90 % recall, but on 150 pairs). No mirror handling.
- **Shen, Efros, Aubry, ArtMiner**, CVPR 2019: 9278–9287 ([CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Shen_Discovering_Visual_Patterns_in_Art_Collections_With_Spatially-Consistent_Feature_Learning_CVPR_2019_paper.html)); IJCV 130(5), 2022, [DOI 10.1007/s11263-022-01576-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-022-01576-x).
- **Shen, Efros, Joulin, Aubry, SegSwap**, CVPR Workshops (IMW) 2022: 5082–5092 ([CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022W/IMW/html/Shen_Learning_Co-Segmentation_by_Segment_Swapping_for_Retrieval_and_Discovery_CVPRW_2022_paper.html)).
- **Chaki et al., "Historical Printed Ornaments"**, ICDAR 2024: 251–270, [arXiv 2408.08633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08633):
  clustering, element discovery and **unsupervised change localisation** between versions, but on
  ornaments, not figurative scenes.
- **VHS** ([ANR-21-CE38-0008](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-21-CE38-0008)), **EIDA** ([ANR-22-CE38-0014](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-22-CE38-0014)), **AIKON** (IJDAR 2026,
  DOI 10.1007/s10032-026-00581-x, full text not read): similarity of scientific illustrations and
  diagrams in manuscripts and prints. **Highest medium-term prior-art risk.**
- **15cILLUSTRATION**:
  - Malaspina & Zhong, *Lettera Matematica* 5(4), 2017, [DOI 10.1007/s40329-017-0201-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40329-017-0201-5);
  - Dondi, Dutta, Malaspina, Zisserman 2020, [DOI 10.30687/978-88-6969-332-8/030](https://doi.org/10.30687/978-88-6969-332-8/030);
  - search tool for block reuse in 15th-century Venetian editions, no mirror handling;
  - its 16th-century follow-up ("1516") is a risk to watch.
- Other references:
  - EBBA Arch-V (no evaluation paper found);
  - Fleuron (Wilkinson et al., DHQ 15.1, 2021);
  - Seguin et al., VISART 2016, [DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-46604-0_52](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-46604-0_52);
  - Lang & Ommer 2018 (DSH 33.4; ECCVW 2018);
  - Zhou & Kong, AVSS 2025, [arXiv 2506.23132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23132): painting plagiarism is easy to recognise (97 %), hard to source (AP 29 %).
- **Emblems and vision**: only *Machina Emblematica* (DH2026; [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/22250825)), a CLIP-based conversational assistant over Camerarius 1668, with neither editions nor copies.

## Pose in art

- **Human-Art** (Ju et al., CVPR 2023, [arXiv 2303.02760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02760)).
  ViTPose AP on Human-Art val, COCO-only → Human-Art + COCO (MMPose config
  [`vitpose_humanart.md`](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmpose/blob/main/configs/body_2d_keypoint/topdown_heatmap/humanart/vitpose_humanart.md),
  read directly):
  - with ground-truth boxes: ViTPose-H 0.665 → 0.800, ViTPose-B 0.555 → 0.759;
  - with detected boxes: ViTPose-H 0.377 → 0.468, ViTPose-B 0.270 → 0.410.

  **Person detection, not pose, is the bottleneck.**
- Other works: Impett & Süsstrunk, ECCV Workshops 2016: 888–902, [DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-46604-0_61](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-46604-0_61); Madhu et al., ACM JOCCH 16, [DOI 10.1145/3569089](https://doi.org/10.1145/3569089); Springstein et al., ACM MM 2022, [arXiv 2207.02976](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02976), and PoPArt ([arXiv 2301.05124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05124)).
- **No pose benchmark for woodcuts or engravings** was found.

## Dense features and segmentation

- DINOv3: Siméoni et al., [arXiv 2508.10104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10104), 2025. Gains on Met and AmsterTime are reported in a search snippet, not checked in the PDF.
- DIFT: Tang et al., NeurIPS 2023, ["Emergent Correspondence from Image Diffusion"](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/0503f5dce343a1d06d16ba103dd52db1-Abstract.html).
- SAM 3: Carion et al., [arXiv 2511.16719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16719), 2025.

None of these is evaluated on woodcuts.

## Gap

1. A **multi-class typed relation** (same block / reversed copy / same-direction copy / free copy /
   new design) evaluated against an **expert ground truth over a long series of printed editions**.
2. Explicit **mirror handling**: absent from the print literature found.
3. **What persists** across versions (pose, composition, objects), measured.
4. **Text or previous picture?** No computational precedent; Tung provides labels.

## Prior-art risks to watch

- Aubry's group and its projects (VHS, AIKON, EIDA).
- The Oxford "1516" project.
- Possible work on Bernard Salomon's copies (Ovid, Bible); none found.
- Still open from v0.3: Cummings 2007, Tung 2012/2013 (library).

## Additions (2026-10-13, second check of tools suggested by ChatGPT; primary sources)

All 15 suggested items exist; some details differ from the suggestion.

**Copy and reuse detection in prints — must be cited:**
- **Lyon16ci** (Tramelli & Dutta, VGG; Biblissima, then VeDPH Ca' Foscari):
  - about 10,000 Lyon illustrations printed 1480–1600, searched with VISE (local keypoints, RANSAC, region search, overlay "toggle");
  - B. Tramelli, *Umanistica Digitale* 21 (2025), [doi:10.60923/issn.2532-8816/21185](https://doi.org/10.60923/issn.2532-8816/21185) (read in full); project page robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/research/16ci/lyon/; book *Indexing the Early Modern Printed Image* (Ca' Foscari, [doi:10.30687/978-88-6969-879-8](https://doi.org/10.30687/978-88-6969-879-8));
  - goal: tracing the reuse of identical images. No copy typology, **no mirror handling** (SIFT-type descriptors are not flip-invariant), no quantitative evaluation;
  - Alciato not mentioned (whether Lyon Alciato editions are in the corpus is unchecked).
  - **Prior-art risk: medium on the material, low on the contribution.** To do: query the demo with a Lyon Alciato cut, upright and mirrored.
- Bergel, Dutta, Zimmermann, "Visual Analysis of Chapbooks Printed in Scotland", HIP 2021 (woodblock reuse and copying).
- Kräutli, Lockhorst, Valleriani, "Calculating Sameness", DSH 2021.

**Correspondence and pose (for "what persists"):**
- MATCHA: Xue et al., CVPR 2025, [arXiv 2501.14945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14945), code github.com/feixue94/matcha. Unified descriptor (diffusion + DINOv2).
- MARCO: Cuttano et al., CVPR 2026, [arXiv 2604.18267](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18267), code github.com/visinf/MARCO. Semantic keypoints on unseen categories.
- Shape-of-You: Im, Liu, Hong, CVPR 2026, [arXiv 2603.11618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11618). Fused Gromov–Wasserstein; DINOv2 + SD + SAM + VGGT.
- Diffusion Hyperfeatures: Luo et al., NeurIPS 2023, [arXiv 2305.14334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14334).
- **Telling Left from Right**: Zhang et al., CVPR 2024, [arXiv 2311.17034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17034). Foundation features confuse left and right: directly relevant to mirrored copies.
- SOCO: Dünkel et al., ECCV 2026, [arXiv 2605.31597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31597). Part-level correspondence benchmark.
- SDPose: Liang et al., [arXiv 2509.24980](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24980) v3 (2026), code github.com/T-S-Liang/SDPose-OOD (MIT). Diffusion-based pose; evaluated on HumanArt and COCO-OOD; only a non-archival ECCV 2026 workshop is confirmed as venue. Not tested on woodcuts.
- SketchFusion: Koley et al., CVPR 2025, [arXiv 2503.14129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14129). Sketch-specific features: possibly suited to line woodcuts.
- Grounded SAM 2 (IDEA-Research) + SAHI (Akyon et al., ICIP 2022): text-prompted detection and segmentation, tiling for small objects.

**Art search systems (similarity, no copy typing):**
- Motif-Match: Lamqaddam et al., DH2025, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.19682677](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19682677). Weighted pose / object composition / colour; no code found.
- Ufer, Simon, Lang, Ommer, PLOS ONE 16 (2021) e0259718. Style-invariant motif search.
- MosAIc: Hamilton et al., [arXiv 2007.07177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.07177).
- iART: Springstein et al., ACM MM 2021.
- ArtSeek: [arXiv 2507.21917](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21917). Artwork understanding, not visual search.
- Rijksmuseum / Q42 blog (26/01/2026): SAM segments + **DINOv2** (not DINOv3), student prototype.
- Visual Contagions (Joyeux-Prunel, UNIGE): periodicals 1797–2021, clustering. Low prior-art risk.
