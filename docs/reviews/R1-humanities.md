# Review 1 — humanities reviewer (*Word & Image* / *Emblematica*; subagent, 2026-10-05), on PROBLEM.md v0.1

Verdict: **major revision, close to reject** as a humanities submission. Observations on 006, 036,
059, 083 checked against the contact sheets and held. Objections:

1. BLOCKING — catalogue "text leakage" is most likely **record inheritance**: A31b036's alt-text is
   A34b035's with the plural removed and its Iconclass-image list a subset of 1534's; A31b083's is
   A34b084's with "Three" → "Two". A31b096 shows cataloguers sometimes did look. Measure string
   inheritance across editions, recover Glasgow's cataloguing order; reframe as record propagation.
2. BLOCKING — the "fixed text" premise is false: 1531 text corrupt, revised 1534; 1550 reordered
   into loci communes; 1621 prints Mignault/Sánchez/Pignoria commentaries (Thuilius) and Tozzi's
   1618 cuts. 1621 Isis with a ship (036) is antiquarian/commentary iconography (Pignoria), not the
   epigram. Code each programme against the text it printed, plus lemma, commentary and visual
   tradition (Greek Anthology, Horapollo, Erasmus' *Adagia*, stock iconography). H3: convergence
   to *which* source?
3. BLOCKING — "fidelity"/"loss" anachronistic: Steyner's *notulae rudiores*; Miedema (emblema =
   epigram); Mignault's *corpus*/*animus*. H1(a) is true by construction. Period-justified
   criterion: errors in the *res picta* (Alciato 1532, Hunger 1542). Use Scholz's notation model;
   drop "loss" as headline.
4. BLOCKING — originality unverified: Cummings 2007, Tung 2012/2013, Scholz 1984/1991, Manning
   1989, Adams 1990, Balavoine 1982, Klecker 2007, Saunders, Russell, Daly. Several "operations"
   are standard emblem-studies knowledge relabelled. Narrow the claim: reliability-checked coding
   plus copy-controlled comparison.
5. BLOCKING — 1531 panel badly specified: A31b is the second Steyner edition (6 April 1531), A31a
   (28 Feb.) the first; woodcut count inconsistent (98 here, 97 Glasgow, 96 Tung). Justify the
   choice, collate A31a/A31b, build a **copy matrix** (block reuse, reversed copy, free copy, new
   invention) before H2/H3.
6. NON-BLOCKING — H2 near-tautological; use stock conventions as baseline or test Scholz's
   prediction and quantify exceptions.
7. NON-BLOCKING — segmentation of a Latin epigram (allusion, irony, refutation): Latinist

8. NON-BLOCKING — operations attribute intentions (096 may be a stock Cupid from the lemma). Code

9. NON-BLOCKING — three papers in one; suggests a humanities article on the res picta with copy
