# In vitam humanam — "On human life"

Editions: [A31b095](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A31b095) · [A34b096](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A34b096) · [A50a151](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A50a151) · [A21a152](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A21a152)

**Text.** Apostrophe: "Weep now, Heraclitus, even more… and you, Democritus, laugh more… I consider
how far I can weep with you, laugh bitterly with you." Comparatives over time (more than before),
named philosophers, the speaker's own divided stance.

**All editions.** Two men, one with hand to face (weeping), one gesturing or laughing (1531: at a
desk; 1534: in a room; 1550/1621: outdoors).

**Observation.** Names are lost (**particular → generic**: "two men"); weeping vs laughing is
readable only weakly (1531 is ambiguous); "even more than before" and the poet's position are
unshown. Compare A31b088, where the image *adds* a name: the direction of the generic/particular
shift is not fixed.
