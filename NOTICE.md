# Attribution and reuse

This repository is a research work in progress. Public availability does not by itself grant an
open-source or open-data licence. No blanket reuse licence has been selected for the project's
original code, notes and annotations.

## Sources and third-party material

- **Alciato at Glasgow**, University of Glasgow: digitised editions, transcriptions,
  translations, annotations, catalogue descriptions and Iconclass indexing.
  [Source website](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/index.php);
  [copyright and reproduction statement](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/#copyright).
  The statement requires express permission to reproduce images. Source downloads and image
  derivatives are excluded from this repository.
- **Mason Tung, *The Variorum Edition of Alciato's Emblemata***:
  [edition and author credit](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/tung-variorum.php).
  The relation tables' `evidence` fields contain excerpts or condensed readings of Tung's
  commentary, cited to support the coded relations. They are third-party source evidence,
  not original project prose or a newly licensed edition of his work. Full PDFs and extracted
  commentaries remain in the local source cache.
- Other scholarly quotations and references are attributed in the research notes and
  [`references.bib`](references.bib). Third-party quotations retain their respective rights.
- Dependencies and downloaded model weights retain their own licences. Model weights are not
  bundled with the repository.

The tables and model outputs are exploratory research artifacts. Machine-produced labels have
not yet been checked by human annotators; see the [current status](README.md#status-and-preliminary-results).
Do not treat them as an adjudicated reconstruction of Alciato's illustration history.
