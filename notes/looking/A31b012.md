# Amicitia etiam post mortem durans — "Friendship lasting beyond death"

Editions: [A31b012](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A31b012) · [A34b012](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A34b012) · [A50a159](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A50a159) · [A21a160](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A21a160)

**Text.** A leafy vine embraces an elm "dried up with age and bare"; the grateful vine repays its
nurse; seek friends whom "not even our final day" will part. Time (age, the past rearing), a
negated future, a moral addressed to "us".

**All editions.** A bare, dead-branched tree wrapped by a vine heavy with grapes: very stable.

**Observation.** Time and death are rendered by **co-presence of contrasting states** (dead tree /
living vine), as force and persistence were in A31b057. The negated future ("not even death will
part them") is carried by that co-presence; the gratitude and the history of rearing are lost.
