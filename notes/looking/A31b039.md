# Tandem tandem iusticia obtinet — "At last justice prevails"

Editions: [A31b039](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A31b039) · [A34b038](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A34b038) · [A50a028](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A50a028) · [A21a028](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A21a028)

**Text.** Achilles' arms were unjustly awarded to Ulysses; Neptune carried the shield, after a
shipwreck, to Ajax's tomb; the wave **speaks**: "Son of Telamon, you have conquered." Long past
narrative, "at last" (tandem), speech, justice as an abstraction.

**1531.** A building (tomb?) on the shore and a round object (shield?) in the water — hard to read.
**1534.** A tomb inscribed **AIACIS**, the shield floating towards it. **1550/1621.** Tomb on the
shore, shield beached by the waves.

**Observation.** "At last" is reduced to **arrival/proximity** (shield touching the tomb); the
wave's speech and the whole backstory vanish. Inscription again supplies the proper name.
