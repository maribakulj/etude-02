# Maturandum — "Make haste slowly"

Editions: [A31b053](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A31b053) · [A34b052](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A34b052) · [A50a020](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A50a020) · [A21a020](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A21a020)

**Text.** Neither impetuous nor too slow: a missile joined with a sucking-fish (remora): the fish
is slow, arrows fast. A paradox built on two **properties of speed**, and a double negation.

**1531.** A blob (Glasgow's alt-text: "a slug") on a horizontal arrow in flight. **1534–1621.** A
fish/eel coiled round a **vertical arrow stuck in the ground**.

**Observation.** Speed is not depictable; the image juxtaposes two objects whose **known
properties** (remora = slow, arrow = fast) must be supplied by the viewer. Later editions even
immobilise the arrow. *Method note:* here the Glasgow alt-text follows the **image** ("slug"), not
the text ("sucking-fish") — the catalogue layer is not uniformly text-driven; this must be
measured, not assumed.
