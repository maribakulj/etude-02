# Mutuum auxilium — "Mutual help"

Editions: [A31b023](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A31b023) · [A34b022](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A34b022) · [A50a160](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A50a160) · [A21a161](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A21a161)

**Text.** A blind man carries on his shoulders a lame man, in return for the use of his eyes; "one
borrows eyes, the other feet". Reciprocity, exchange, an agreement.

**1531.** Two men **walking side by side**, one with a wooden leg, the other with a stick — no
carrying. **1534–1621.** The blind man carries the lame man, who **points ahead** (1550, 1621):
the pointing gesture stands for "lending his eyes".

**Observation.** Control case: concrete and depictable. The composite body and the pointing render
the exchange well once the cutter follows the text; 1531 again departs from the text's key
configuration.
