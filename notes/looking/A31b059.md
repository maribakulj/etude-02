# Aliquid mali propter vicinum malum — "Some harm from a bad neighbour"

Editions: [A31b059](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A31b059) · [A34b058](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A34b058) · [A50a165](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A50a165) · [A21a166](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A21a166)

**Text.** Two pots carried by a stream, one metal, one clay; the metal one proposes to float side
by side; the clay one **replies** that *whether* the wave pushes you against me *or* me against
you, I alone will break. Dialogue, a disjunctive conditional about the future, and a contrast of
**materials**.

**All editions.** Two pots in a river. 1531: a handled bucket and a jar, differently shaped.
**1621: two near-identical ceramic jugs** — the material contrast on which the whole argument rests
is no longer visible.

**Observation.** The image fixes position and form; the dialogue, the hypothetical collision and
its asymmetric outcome are absent. Material (metal vs clay) is depictable only through hatching and
form conventions, and **drift can erase it**. Candidate category: the image must commit to a
property the text names (material), and later copies may lose it.
