# In adulatores — "On flatterers" (chameleon)

Editions: [A31b087](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A31b087) (no woodcut) · [A34b088](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A34b088) · [A50a053](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A50a053) · [A21a053](https://www.emblems.arts.gla.ac.uk/alciato/emblem.php?id=A21a053)

**Text.** The chameleon feeds on air with open mouth and changes into **all colours except red and
white**; so the flatterer imitates only the prince's black features. Colour, change, an exception.

**1531.** No woodcut. **1534.** A scaly quadruped with a pig's snout; **1550.** a hedgehog-like
beast; **1621.** a recognisable chameleon on a branch.

**Observation.** Two kinds of "cannot": **change over time** (picture-generic) and **colour** in a
monochrome woodcut (**medium-specific**). The exception "except red and white" is doubly
unreachable. Distinguish in the scheme: what no single picture can show vs what this medium
(black-line woodcut) cannot. Also: the animal itself is unknown to the early cutters (zoological
knowledge as a factor).
